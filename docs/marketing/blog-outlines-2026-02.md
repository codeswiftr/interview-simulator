## 2. Why the STAR Method is Broken (and How to Fix It)
**Target Audience:** Candidates who feel their answers are too "robotic" or dry.
**SEO Keywords:** STAR method, STAR interview technique, behavioral interview framework.
**Goal:** Introduce the "Conflict-First" or "Action-Heavy" variation of STAR.

### Outline:
*   **Introduction:**
    *   The "Safe" choice: Why everyone uses STAR and why it’s becoming a background noise for interviewers.
    *   The "Robot" Problem: Sounding like a script.
*   **The Structural Flaw:**
    *   Burying the lead: Spending 60 seconds on "Situation" when the interviewer only wants the "Action."
*   **The STAR 2.0 Framework:**
    *   **S/T (Context):** Keep it under 15 seconds. High stakes only.
    *   **A (The Meat):** Expand this to 70% of your answer. Show your reasoning/trade-offs.
    *   **R (The Result):** Quantify it. "Reduced latency by X%" or "Saved Y hours."
*   **Adding the "Learned" (STAR-L):**
    *   The most important part for Senior/Staff roles: What would you do differently next time?
*   **Conclusion & CTA:**
    *   Break the formula, keep the structure.
    *   **CTA:** Get an objective "STAR Score" on your practice sessions at app.codeswiftr.com.

## 3. The 7 Deadly Sins of Modern Tech Interviews
**Target Audience:** Active job seekers wondering why they aren't getting past the final round.
**SEO Keywords:** interview mistakes, failing technical interviews, interview feedback.
**Goal:** High-engagement "listicle" that addresses common blind spots.

### Outline:
*   **Introduction:**
    *   You have the skills, you have the resume. Why no offer?
*   **The Mistakes:**
    1.  **The "We" Trap:** Talking about the team instead of your specific contribution.
    2.  **Monotone Delivery:** Low energy reads as lack of interest over Zoom.
    3.  **The Silent Coder:** Not thinking out loud during the technical round.
    4.  **Bad-Mouthing Past Employers:** Even if it’s true, it’s a red flag.
    5.  **Ignoring the "Non-Technical" Cues:** Not asking clarifying questions on system design.
    6.  **The "Everything is Great" Lie:** Claiming you’ve never had a conflict or failure.
    7.  **Poor Remote Setup:** Bad audio/lighting sabotages your "executive presence."
*   **How to Audit Yourself:**
    *   Record and playback: The "cringe" test.
*   **Conclusion & CTA:**
    *   Self-awareness is 50% of the battle.
