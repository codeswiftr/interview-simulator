## Executive Summary

This playbook provides a tactical roadmap to acquire your first 5 paying customers for Interview Simulator. Based on competitive analysis (Interviewing.io, Pramp) and SaaS pricing psychology research, the focus is on **high-intent, low-friction channels** where software engineers actively seek interview help.

**Key Insight:** Interview prep is a high-anxiety, time-sensitive need. Your first customers are likely actively interviewing RIGHT NOW, not planning for interviews 3 months from now.

## PART 1: Top 5 Channels to Find First 5 Customers

### Channel 1: Reddit Communities (Highest Priority)

**Why:** r/cscareerquestions has 1M+ members actively discussing interviews. Engineers here are in job-seeking mode.

**Target Subreddits:**

| Subreddit | Members | Best Time to Post | Approach |
|-----------|---------|-------------------|----------|
| **r/cscareerquestions** | 1M+ | Tue-Thu 9-11am EST | Value-first comments |
| **r/leetcode** | 750K+ | Weekend mornings | "Show HN" style post |
| **r/ExperiencedDevs** | 400K+ | Weekday lunch | Target senior engineers |
| **r/learnprogramming** | 5M+ | Weekends | Junior dev focus |
| **r/SWE** | 150K+ | Any weekday | Direct posts allowed |

**Specific Strategy - "The Mock Interview Gap":**

1. **Listen First:** Search for posts with keywords: "mock interview," "practice interview," "interview prep," "nervous about interview"

2. **Value-First Comments (DO NOT SELL DIRECTLY):**
   ```
   "I had the same problem before my Google onsite. What helped me was 
   practicing with timed AI mocks to reduce anxiety. The key is getting 
   feedback on communication, not just coding."
   ```

3. **DM Strategy (After 2+ helpful comments):**
   - Wait for OP to reply to your comment
   - Send DM: "Saw your post about interview anxiety. I built something 
     that might help - happy to give you free access if you want to try 
     it. No pitch, just want feedback from someone in the trenches."

4. **"Show HN" Style Post (After 3-5 organic customers):**
   - Post title: "I built an AI interview simulator after failing 4 FAANG onsites"
   - Lead with personal story
   - End with: "Looking for 5 beta users who are interviewing in the next 2 weeks"

**Expected Conversion:** 1 customer per 20-30 helpful interactions

## PART 3: Pricing Psychology - $X vs $Y Monthly

### Competitive Pricing Analysis

| Competitor | Pricing Model | Monthly Equivalent |
|------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Interviewing.io** | $200+ per mock | ~$800/mo (4 mocks) |
| **Pramp** | Free (peer-to-peer) | $0 |
| **Interview Coder AI** | $29/month | $29/mo |
| **Thita.ai** | Free tier + Premium | $29/mo |
| **General coaching** | $150-300/hour | ~$600-1200/mo |

### Pricing Psychology Principles

#### 1. Price Anchoring

**Strategy:** Show the expensive alternative first

```
Traditional mock interviews: $200/session × 4 = $800/month
Interview Simulator: $39/month

You save: $761/month
```

**Why it works:** The human brain anchors to the first number ($800). Everything after seems cheap by comparison.

#### 2. Left-Digit Effect

**Strategy:** Use $29 instead of $30, $49 instead of $50

```
$29/month reads as "twenty-something"
$30/month reads as "thirty"

Perception: $29 is ~13% cheaper psychologically
```

#### 3. Decoy Effect (Three-Tier Pricing)

```
┌─────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
│   Basic     │   Pro ⭐     │   Premium    │
├─────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┤
│   $19/mo    │   $39/mo     │   $79/mo     │
│             │              │              │
│ 5 mocks/mo  │ Unlimited    │ Unlimited    │
│ Basic FB    │ Priority FB  │ 1:1 Coaching │
│             │              │ (1x month)   │
└─────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┘
```

**Why it works:** The middle option ($39) looks like a bargain compared to Premium, while Basic looks too limited. Most customers choose Pro.

#### 4. Annual Pricing Psychology

**Recommended Structure:**

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Savings |
|------|---------|--------|---------|
| **Basic** | $19 | $190/year (2 months free) | 16% |
| **Pro** | $39 | $390/year (2 months free) | 16% |
| **Premium** | $79 | $590/year (6 months free) | 37% |

**Display like this:**
```
$39/month
or $390/year (save $78)
```

**Why it works:** Annual plans improve cash flow and reduce churn. Frame as "X months free" not "X% off" - it's more tangible.

#### 5. The "Cost of Failure" Frame

**Reframe your price against the cost of not getting the job:**

```
Missed FAANG offer: $150,000+ in lost first-year compensation
Interview Simulator: $39/month

ROI: If it helps you land just ONE offer, you get 3,846× return
```
