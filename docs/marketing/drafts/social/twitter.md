## Tweet 1: Anxiety Hack

Interview anxiety hack that actually works:

Double inhale through nose
Long exhale through mouth

Takes 10 seconds.
Activates your parasympathetic nervous system.
Stops the panic.

Neuroscience, not woo-woo.

## Tweet 2: STAR Method Fix

STAR method is killing your behavioral interviews.

The problem: You bury the interesting part under "situation" context.

The fix: Start with conflict.

"Everyone said it was impossible" > "The situation was our team was working on..."

Same story. Different impact.

## Tweet 3: 90-Second Rule

The 90-second rule for interview answers:

0-10 sec: Conflict setup
10-70 sec: What YOU did
70-90 sec: Result + learning

Under 60 sec = hiding something
Over 2 min = losing them

Practice with a timer.

## Tweet 4: Reframe Nervousness

Stop saying "I'm nervous" in interviews.

Say: "I'm excited about this opportunity."

Same physiological state.
Completely different framing.

Your brain believes what you tell it.

## Tweet 7: Salary Negotiation

Salary negotiation in 30 seconds:

1. Never accept on the spot
2. Ask for 48 hours to review
3. Have a specific number (not a range)
4. Negotiate by phone, not email
5. Always ask—they expect it

Average increase: 10-15%

Stop leaving money on the table.

## Tweet 10: Behavioral Checklist

The best behavioral interview answers include:

✓ Conflict (stakes)
✓ Specifics (numbers)
✓ Reasoning (why that choice)
✓ Failure (what you learned)
✓ Self-awareness (what you'd do differently)

Perfection is suspicious.
Reality is compelling.

## Thread: 2-Week Interview Prep

**Thread Indicator:** Use this as a thread for higher engagement

🧵 1/8
Interview in 2 weeks? Here's the exact plan I'd follow.

No fluff. Just what works.

---

2/8 WEEK 1 - FOUNDATION

Day 1-2: Write + practice 5 behavioral stories (2h/day)
Day 3-4: Master 5 core coding patterns (2h/day)
Day 5: Company research (1h)

---

3/8 WEEK 1 - WEEKEND

System design basics (4h total)

Focus on:
- Load balancers
- Databases (SQL vs NoSQL)
- Caching strategies
- API design

Don't go deep. Go wide.

---

4/8 WEEK 2 - PRACTICE

Day 8-9: Mock interviews (2h/day)
Day 10: Address gaps from mocks (2h)

Mocks reveal what you don't know.
That's the point.

---

5/8 WEEK 2 - POLISH

Day 11-12: Polish and refine (1.5h/day)

Focus on your weakest area from mocks.
Don't add new material.

---

6/8 WEEK 2 - FINAL PREP

Weekend: Final mock + logistics

Check your setup:
- Camera
- Audio
- Background
- Backup plans

---

7/8 INTERVIEW DAY

Light exercise
Good meal
NO new studying

Your brain needs rest, not cramming.

---

8/8 THE KEY INSIGHT

Total time: 20 hours

Behavioral prep has the highest ROI.
It's where most engineers are weakest.
And it's faster to improve than algorithms.

Good luck. You've got this.
