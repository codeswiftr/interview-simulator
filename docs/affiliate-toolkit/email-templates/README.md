# Email Templates for Affiliates

These email templates are ready to customize and send to your audience. Replace `[YOUR_LINK]` with your affiliate link.

## Available Templates

| Template | Best For | Tone |
|----------|----------|------|
| [Newsletter Mention](./01-newsletter-mention.md) | Newsletter subscribers | Casual, helpful |
| [Dedicated Promo](./02-dedicated-promo.md) | Product-focused email | Direct, persuasive |
| [Personal Story](./03-personal-story.md) | Personal brand audiences | Authentic, relatable |
| [Career Advice](./04-career-advice.md) | Career coaching lists | Educational |
| [Follow-Up](./05-follow-up.md) | Re-engagement | Gentle reminder |

## Best Practices

1. **Personalize the subject line** - Add recipient's name if possible
2. **Add your own experience** - Share how you've used the product
3. **Include a clear CTA** - One primary call-to-action per email
4. **Disclose affiliate relationship** - Required by FTC guidelines
5. **Segment your list** - Send to people interested in tech careers

## FTC Disclosure

Always include a disclosure like:
> "This email contains affiliate links. If you sign up through my link, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you."
