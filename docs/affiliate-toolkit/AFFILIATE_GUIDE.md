## Best Practices

### Do's ✅

1. **Be authentic** - Share your genuine experience
2. **Add value first** - Teach something useful, then recommend
3. **Target the right audience** - Software engineers, career changers, bootcamp grads
4. **Use multiple channels** - Don't rely on just one platform
5. **Track your links** - Use UTM parameters to see what works
6. **Disclose your relationship** - Required by FTC

### Don'ts ❌

1. **Don't spam** - Quality over quantity
2. **Don't make false claims** - "Guaranteed job" etc.
3. **Don't bid on brand terms** - No PPC for "Interview Simulator"
4. **Don't use misleading links** - Be transparent about affiliate relationship
5. **Don't mass DM** - This hurts your reputation

## Tracking Your Performance

### Dashboard Metrics

Log in to your affiliate dashboard to see:

- **Clicks**: People who clicked your link
- **Visitors**: Unique visitors from your link
- **Leads**: People who signed up
- **Conversions**: People who upgraded to Pro
- **Revenue**: Your commission earnings

### UTM Tracking

Add UTM parameters to track which content performs best:

```
https://app.codeswiftr.com?via=YOUR_CODE&utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=interview-tips
```

Parameters:
- `utm_source`: Where traffic comes from (blog, twitter, linkedin)
- `utm_medium`: Type of content (post, email, video)
- `utm_campaign`: Specific campaign name

## Common Questions

### When do I get paid?

Payments are processed monthly on the 15th for the previous month's confirmed commissions. Minimum payout is $50.

### What payment methods are available?

PayPal and Wise (formerly TransferWise).

### How long does the cookie last?

60 days. If someone clicks your link and converts within 60 days, you get credit.

### What if someone cancels?

You only earn commission on payments actually made. If a customer cancels, future commissions stop but you keep what you've already earned.

### Can I promote on paid ads?

Yes, except:
- No bidding on "Interview Simulator" or "CodeSwiftr" brand terms
- No misleading ad copy
- Must comply with platform ad policies
