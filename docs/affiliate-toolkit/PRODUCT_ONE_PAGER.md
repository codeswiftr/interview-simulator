# Interview Simulator - Product One-Pager

Use this document to quickly understand and explain the product to your audience.

---

## The Problem

Software engineers spend 100+ hours preparing for coding interviews but barely practice behavioral questions.

Then they freeze when asked "Tell me about a time..."

**The gap:** People think about answers but don't practice speaking them out loud.

---

## The Solution

**Interview Simulator** is an AI-powered platform that helps engineers practice behavioral interviews by:

1. **Recording answers** to real interview questions
2. **Providing instant AI feedback** on content, structure, and delivery
3. **Tracking improvement** over time

Think of it as a gym for interview skills - practice in private, perform in interviews.

## Common Objections & Responses

### "I can just practice with friends"

Friends are busy, feedback is awkward, and scheduling is a pain. Interview Simulator is available 24/7 with objective, detailed feedback every time.

### "AI feedback can't be that good"

The AI catches things humans miss (filler words, rambling, missing details) and things humans are too polite to mention. Try 5 free sessions - you'll see.

### "I don't have time"

One session takes 10-15 minutes. Practice on your commute or lunch break. Small daily practice beats one long cram session.

### "I already know my stories"

Knowing your stories and articulating them clearly under pressure are different skills. Recording yourself reveals the gap.
