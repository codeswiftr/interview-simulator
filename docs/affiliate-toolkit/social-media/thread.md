### Tweet/Post 2
First, understand what interviewers actually want:

They're not looking for the "right" answer.

They want to see:
- How you think
- How you communicate
- Whether you'll be good to work with

Your stories are just vehicles for demonstrating these.

---

### Tweet/Post 3
The STAR method exists for a reason.

Situation - 1 sentence context
Task - What YOU needed to do
Action - What YOU did (specific!)
Result - Measurable outcome

But here's what most people miss:

The "Action" section should be 50-60% of your answer.

---

### Tweet/Post 4
Common mistakes I see:

❌ Too much context (2 min of background)
❌ Using "we" instead of "I"
❌ Vague actions ("I helped the team")
❌ No measurable results
❌ Going over 2-3 minutes

Each one can tank an otherwise good answer.

---

### Tweet/Post 5
The only way to catch these mistakes?

Practice out loud. Recorded.

Not in your head. Not typing.

Actually speaking like you would in an interview.

I know it feels weird. Do it anyway.

---

### Tweet/Post 6
Here's my practice system:

1. Pick 10 common behavioral questions
2. Write bullet points for each (not scripts)
3. Practice each one 3x, recorded
4. Listen back, critique yourself
5. Practice until it feels natural

Takes about 1-2 hours total. Worth every minute.
