## Post 6: Value-First Long Form

How to answer "Tell me about a time you failed" (without tanking your interview):

Most candidates do this:

- Pick a small, safe "failure"
- Blame external factors
- Rush to the happy ending

Interviewers see through this instantly.

Here's the better approach:

1. Choose a REAL failure (yes, really)
2. Own it completely - no excuses
3. Focus 70% on what you LEARNED
4. Show how you've CHANGED

Example structure:
"At [Company], I [specific failure]. Looking back, I [what you did wrong]. This taught me [lesson]. Since then, I've [how you've changed], which is why [recent success]."

The key: Your failure should make your growth believable.
