# Social Media Copy for Affiliates

Ready-to-post content for various platforms. Replace `[LINK]` with your affiliate link.

## Platform Guidelines

| Platform | Character Limit | Best Format | Hashtag Limit |
|----------|-----------------|-------------|---------------|
| LinkedIn | 3,000 | Long-form, professional | 3-5 |
| Twitter/X | 280 | Concise, punchy | 1-2 |
| Instagram | 2,200 | Story-driven | 5-10 |
| Facebook | 63,206 | Medium length | 1-3 |

## Available Templates

- [LinkedIn Posts](./linkedin.md) - Professional, career-focused
- [Twitter/X Posts](./twitter.md) - Short, engaging
- [Instagram Captions](./instagram.md) - Visual-first, authentic
- [Thread Template](./thread.md) - Multi-post format

## Tips for Success

1. **Add your voice** - Personalize these templates
2. **Post at peak times** - LinkedIn: Tue-Thu 8-10am, Twitter: 12-3pm
3. **Engage with comments** - Reply to everyone
4. **Use visuals** - Posts with images get 2x engagement
5. **Disclose affiliate relationship** - Use #ad or #affiliate

## FTC Compliance

Always disclose your affiliate relationship. Options:
- Add "#ad" or "#affiliate" to the post
- Say "affiliate link" in the caption
- Use platform's paid partnership tag if available
