## Design Guidelines

### Do's
- Use clean, professional design
- Keep text minimal and readable
- Include clear CTA button
- Use brand colors consistently
- Ensure mobile readability

### Don'ts
- Don't use stock photos of fake "tech workers"
- Don't make false claims (guaranteed job, etc.)
- Don't use more than 2-3 colors
- Don't use small text that's hard to read
- Don't overcrowd with features

## CTA Button Text Options

- "Try Free"
- "Start Practicing"
- "Practice Now"
- "Get Started Free"
- "Try 5 Free Sessions"
