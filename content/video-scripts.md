## Script 1: "Why STAR Fails You" (60 sec)

**[HOOK – 0:00]**  
"Stop using the STAR method."

**[CONFLICT – 0:03]**  
Seriously. Every candidate uses it. Every answer sounds the same. Interviewers can tell you memorized it.

**[TWIST – 0:08]**  
The problem isn't STAR itself. It's how you use it.

You're spending 5 minutes on Situation and 30 seconds on Result.

**[THE FIX – 0:15]**  
Flip it. Quick situation—one sentence. Then 60% of your answer goes to Action. What *you* did. Your ownership. Your decisions.

End with numbers. "Reduced latency 40%." "Saved 50K a year."

## Script 2: "The One Question That Predicts Your Offer" (75 sec)

**[HOOK – 0:00]**  
"There's one question that predicts whether you'll get the offer."

**[BUILD – 0:03]**  
It's not "Tell me about yourself." It's not a coding question.

It's this: "Tell me about a time you failed."

**[WHY – 0:10]**  
Because how you answer shows three things:  
One—can you own your mistakes?  
Two—did you learn from them?  
Three—are you honest or are you hiding?

**[TRAP – 0:20]**  
Most candidates say: "I'm a perfectionist. I work too hard."

That's a red flag. Interviewers have heard it a thousand times.

**[THE FIX – 0:28]**  
Give a real failure. One where you had impact. Explain what you did wrong. What you learned. How you changed. With specifics.

## Script 3: "Mind Blank in Interviews? Here's Why" (60 sec)

**[HOOK – 0:00]**  
"Your mind goes blank in interviews. It's not because you're dumb."

**[EXPLAIN – 0:03]**  
It's your nervous system. When you're stressed, the part of your brain that *thinks* goes offline. The part that senses danger takes over.

**[KEY INSIGHT – 0:12]**  
More studying won't fix it. You're not underprepared. You're over-stressed.

**[THE FIX – 0:18]**  
You need exposure. Practice under pressure. Over and over. Your brain needs to learn: interviews aren't actually dangerous.

**[CALLBACK – 0:28]**

## Script 4: "Salary Negotiation Script" (90 sec)

**[HOOK – 0:00]**  
"You have more leverage than you think."

**[SETUP – 0:03]**  
When they offer you a number, most candidates say "Okay" or "Can you do better?" Both are weak.

**[THE SCRIPT – 0:10]**  
Here's what works:

"Thank you for the offer. I'm excited about the role. Based on my research and experience, I was expecting something in the [X] to [Y] range. Is there flexibility?"

**[KEY – 0:22]**  
Anchor high. Give a range. Let them come to you.

**[IF THEY PUSH BACK – 0:28]**  
"I understand. What would it take to get to [your number]? Is there room in the budget, or could we look at sign-on, equity, or flexibility?"

**[CLOSING – 0:38]**  
Stay calm. Don't apologize. You're not being greedy—you're having a professional conversation.

## Script 5: "5 Stories You Need for Any Interview" (75 sec)

**[HOOK – 0:00]**  
"You don't need 50 stories. You need 5."

**[THE FIVE – 0:03]**  
One: A complex technical challenge. Depth, ownership, learning.  
Two: Cross-team collaboration. Influence without authority.  
Three: A big mistake. Accountability, growth, what you fixed.  
Four: A process or system you built. Vision, execution, impact.  
Five: A time you disagreed. Conviction, data, outcome.

**[THE TRICK – 0:25]**  
Each story maps to multiple questions. "Tell me about a conflict" → story 2 or 5. "Tell me about a failure" → story 3.

**[PRACTICE – 0:35]**  
Prepare these five. Practice them out loud. Get feedback. Refine. You'll cover 80% of behavioral questions.
