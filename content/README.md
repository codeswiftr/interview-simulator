## Content Production Guidelines

1. **Voice:** Supportive but not patronizing; confident but not arrogant; data-driven but empathetic
2. **Avoid hype language:** Prefer specific outcomes ("3 candidates landed offers after 12 practice sessions") over generic claims ("revolutionary interview prep")
3. **Every piece ends with a CTA:** "Start your free trial," "Download the guide," "Book a demo"
4. **Reuse core assets:** One case study becomes a blog post, 3 social posts, 1 email section, and a landing page testimonial
5. **SEO optimization:** Target keywords in titles, meta descriptions, H2 headers
6. **Metrics must be real or clearly marked as projections**
7. **STAR method content must include templates, not just theory**
