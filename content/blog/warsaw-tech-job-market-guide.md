# Warsaw Tech Job Market: Software Engineer Interview Guide

Warsaw has emerged as one of Central and Eastern Europe's most important tech hubs. Poland's strong engineering universities, EU membership providing access to the European market, and significantly lower costs than Western Europe have attracted major tech companies and a thriving startup ecosystem. This guide covers the Warsaw tech job market for software engineers.

## The Warsaw Tech Ecosystem

Warsaw's tech scene spans multiple sectors with particularly strong depth in gaming, e-commerce, and financial technology:

**Consumer tech and e-commerce**: Allegro (Poland's largest e-commerce platform, NASDAQ listed, similar to Amazon's role in Poland) employs hundreds of engineers and runs sophisticated engineering challenges around marketplace scale. OLX Group (classifieds platform) maintains significant engineering presence in Warsaw.

**Gaming industry**: CD Projekt (The Witcher, Cyberpunk 2077) is one of Poland's most famous companies globally. Poland has a strong game development ecosystem — also Techland (Dying Light), Bloober Team, and dozens of smaller studios cluster around Warsaw and Kraków. Game engineers from Warsaw are globally recognized for their quality.

**Fintech and banking**: mBank (digital banking pioneer in Europe), Revolut's major engineering hub, and ING Tech Poland represent significant fintech engineering presence. Warsaw engineers have deep payment systems and banking software expertise.

**IT services and consulting**: Capgemini, Accenture, and Cognizant maintain large delivery centers in Warsaw. These provide stable employment and exposure to enterprise-scale projects across European markets.

**Startups and growth companies**: Booksy (beauty appointment SaaS, unicorn), Brainly (education platform), Packhelp (packaging e-commerce), and DocPlanner (healthcare appointments) represent Poland's successful product companies. Many have raised significant US/European venture capital.

## Salary Ranges and Compensation

Poland offers competitive salaries relative to cost of living, though below Western European levels:

| Level | Monthly Salary (PLN) | Monthly Salary (EUR equiv.) |
|-------|----------------------|-----------------------------|
| Junior (0-3 years) | 6,000–10,000 PLN | €1,400–€2,300 |
| Mid-level (3-6 years) | 10,000–18,000 PLN | €2,300–€4,200 |
| Senior (6-10 years) | 18,000–30,000 PLN | €4,200–€7,000 |
| Staff/Lead | 28,000–45,000 PLN | €6,500–€10,500 |

**B2B contracting**: Most senior Polish engineers operate on B2B (business-to-business) contracts rather than employment contracts. B2B rates are 20-40% higher than employment salaries due to different tax treatment. Common form is JDG (Jednoosobowa Działalność Gospodarcza — sole proprietorship). Ask for both employment and B2B options when negotiating.

**Remote premium**: Warsaw engineers at US or UK remote companies can earn €6,000–€12,000/month as senior contractors — 2-3× local market rates. Remote work has significantly raised Warsaw's engineering compensation ceiling.

**Cost of living**: Warsaw is significantly cheaper than London, Amsterdam, or Stockholm. A 2-bedroom apartment in Mokotów, Żoliborz, or Praga costs 3,000–6,000 PLN/month (€700–€1,400). This creates strong quality of life relative to compensation.

## Interview Culture and Process

**Allegro**: Strong engineering culture with rigorous interviews. Expect system design (e-commerce at national scale), algorithms, and behavioral rounds. Allegro publishes an engineering blog (allegro.tech) that signals their technical values — read it before interviewing.

**CD Projekt**: Game development interviews differ from standard product companies. Expect C++ proficiency (the game industry standard), knowledge of game engine architectures, rendering pipelines, and performance optimization at the microsecond level. Portfolio of shipped games or notable game projects helps significantly.

**Revolut Poland**: Fintech-standard interview rigor. Algorithms (LeetCode medium/hard), system design (financial systems at scale), and behavioral rounds assessing ownership and bias for action.

**Polish interview culture**: Polish engineers tend to be direct, technically rigorous, and less focused on soft skills signaling than UK or US interviews. Substance over presentation is valued. Strong technical arguments supported by evidence are more persuasive than confident assertions.

**Language**: Most Warsaw tech companies operate in English, especially senior roles and companies with international teams. Polish is helpful for day-to-day interaction but rarely a hard requirement at established tech companies.

## The Polish Engineering Talent Advantage

Understanding what makes Warsaw engineers distinctive helps in interviews:

**Strong academic foundations**: Warsaw University of Technology (Politechnika Warszawska), Warsaw University, and AGH Kraków produce engineers with rigorous CS fundamentals. Poland consistently ranks in the top 10 globally in competitive programming (ICPC, IOI).

**EU market experience**: Working in the EU market means deep familiarity with GDPR, EU payment regulations (PSD2), and multi-currency/multi-language product development. Valuable for companies expanding into Europe.

**Remote work maturity**: Polish engineers have embraced remote work comprehensively, with strong async communication skills and experience navigating timezone collaboration with US and UK teams.

## Preparation Tips

1. Study GDPR and EU data protection requirements — fundamental knowledge for Polish/European companies
2. For game roles: build a portfolio project in Unreal Engine or Unity and be ready to discuss engine internals
3. For Allegro or fintech: study distributed systems and high-availability system design
4. Network through dev.pl community, Warsaw IT meetups, and Polish LinkedIn tech groups
5. Consider B2B contracting setup for senior roles — typically higher net compensation

Warsaw is an excellent tech career destination combining world-class companies (gaming, fintech, e-commerce), growing startup ecosystem, EU lifestyle, and cost of living significantly below Western Europe. The engineering community is strong and internationally respected.
