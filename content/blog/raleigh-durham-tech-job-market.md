# Raleigh-Durham Tech Job Market Guide: The Research Triangle's Tech Renaissance

*Three universities, world-class research, and a cost of living that lets you actually save money.*

---

## Why Raleigh-Durham?

### The Research Triangle Advantage

**Three Tier-1 Research Universities:**
- **Duke University** (Durham)
- **NC State University** (Raleigh)
- **University of North Carolina** (Chapel Hill)

**This means:**
- Steady pipeline of talent
- Research partnerships
- University-affiliated startups
- Lifelong learning opportunities

### The Pros
- **Affordable:** Median home price ~$425K
- **Educated population:** 48% college-educated
- **Diverse economy:** Tech, biotech, healthcare, education
- **Great weather:** Four seasons, mild winters
- **Growing fast:** Population up 23% since 2010

### The Cons
- **Car-dependent:** Limited public transit
- **Sprawl:** Three cities = lots of driving
- **Hot summers:** 90°F+ July-August
- **Competition for talent:** Universities keep wages moderate

## Salary Expectations (2026)

| Level | Raleigh-Durham | Charlotte | Atlanta |
|-------|----------------|-----------|---------|
| **Junior** | $80K-$100K | $85K-$105K | $90K-$110K |
| **Mid-Level** | $110K-$135K | $110K-$140K | $120K-$150K |
| **Senior** | $140K-$170K | $140K-$170K | $150K-$190K |
| **Staff+** | $170K-$220K | $170K-$220K | $190K-$260K |

*Note: Apple's new campus may drive salaries up 10-15%*

### Compensation Philosophy

**Enterprise Software (SAS, Red Hat):**
- Solid base salaries
- Good benefits, work-life balance
- RSUs common at public companies

**Startups (Pendo, etc.):**
- Lower base, equity upside
- More risk, more potential reward

**Big Tech (Apple, Google):**
- Bay Area salaries adjusted for cost of living
- 85-90% of SF pay for 60% of cost

## The Triangle Tech Culture

### Research-Driven

**Biotech Meets Tech:**
- IQVIA, Biogen, GSK all in RTP
- Health tech is huge
- Data science skills transfer well

**Open Source Heritage:**
- Red Hat's influence is everywhere
- Strong Linux community
- Open source contributions valued

### Startup Ecosystem

**Triangle Startup Factory**
- Strong angel network
- University spinoffs common
- More B2B/enterprise than consumer

**Coworking Spaces:**
- American Underground (Durham)
- HQ Raleigh
- WeWork (multiple locations)

### Work Culture

**"Work Hard, Live Well"**
- 40-45 hour weeks standard
- Commutes rarely exceed 30 minutes
- Outdoor culture (hiking, cycling)

**Triangle Tech Deck:**
- Monthly startup showcases
- RTP 180 (entrepreneurship program)
- Strong networking culture

## Interviewing in Raleigh-Durham

### RTP Company Interviews

**SAS-Style (Traditional):**
- Behavioral emphasis
- Work-life balance questions
- Long-term career focus

**Red Hat-Style (Open Source):**
- Collaboration questions
- Open source contribution discussion
- Remote work experience

**Startup-Style (Pendo, etc.):**
- Move fast, break things culture
- Product sense questions
- Equity/ownership mindset

### University Connections

Many companies have university hiring pipelines:
- Fall career fairs are critical
- Research assistantships lead to jobs
- Internship programs very active

## Getting Here

### From College

**Direct pipeline:**
- NC State → RTP companies
- Duke → Biotech/health tech
- UNC → Various

**Internships:**
- Summer programs at SAS, Red Hat
- Research positions at universities
- Startup internships

### From Other Cities

**Remote-first:**
- Many RTP companies went remote
- Can live here, work anywhere
- Lower cost = higher savings rate

**Relocation support:**
- Large companies offer packages
- Startups: usually nothing
- Negotiate for moving costs

## Final Tips

1. **Pick your city:** Raleigh (corporate), Durham (startup/cool), Chapel Hill (college town)
2. **Get a car:** Public transit between cities is limited
3. **Explore the food:** Raleigh's restaurant scene is underrated
4. **Embrace the outdoors:** Umstead State Park is 10 minutes away
5. **Network at universities:** Even if you didn't attend, events are open
