# Cairo Tech Job Market: Software Engineer Interview Guide

Cairo has established itself as North Africa's technology capital and one of the Middle East-North Africa (MENA) region's most dynamic startup ecosystems. With a massive educated population, rapidly growing internet penetration, and government investment in digital infrastructure, Cairo is producing world-class engineers and attracting international tech investment. This guide helps software engineers navigate the Cairo tech job market.

## The Cairo Tech Ecosystem

Cairo's tech sector is characterized by rapid growth and increasing international visibility:

**Startup unicorns and near-unicorns**: Instabug (bug reporting SDK, widely used globally, $100M+ raised), Swvl (transit tech, went public via SPAC), Vezeeta (healthcare platform), and Paymob (payment processing, widely used across MENA) represent Cairo's tech flagship companies. These companies hire engineers at globally competitive standards.

**Fintech and payments**: Egypt's mobile money revolution has driven significant fintech engineering. Fawry (Egypt's dominant digital payments company, EGX listed) processes billions of transactions. Paymob and Khazna (digital banking) are building financial infrastructure for Egypt's largely unbanked population. Payment systems engineers with MENA market knowledge are in high demand globally.

**E-commerce and logistics**: Jumia Egypt (pan-African e-commerce), Rabbit (quick commerce), and Cartona (B2B grocery marketplace) represent Cairo's e-commerce tech stack. Supply chain software, last-mile delivery optimization, and inventory management are recurring technical challenges.

**Global tech offices**: Amazon (AWS Egypt), Microsoft Egypt, and Oracle Egypt maintain significant engineering teams in Cairo. These offer international-standard compensation and career development within their global organizations.

**Remote work explosion**: Egyptian engineers increasingly work remotely for US and European companies via platforms like Andela, Turing, and direct recruitment. Dollar-denominated remote work has become the highest-compensation path for senior Cairo engineers.

## Salary and Compensation

Egypt's inflation and currency devaluation make USD compensation critical for senior engineers:

| Level | EGP Monthly (Local) | USD Monthly (Remote/International) |
|-------|---------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Junior (0-3 years) | 8,000–18,000 EGP | $800–$1,500 |
| Mid-level (3-6 years) | 18,000–40,000 EGP | $1,500–$4,000 |
| Senior (6+ years) | 40,000–80,000 EGP | $4,000–$10,000 |
| Staff/Lead | 70,000–150,000 EGP | $8,000–$18,000 |

**EGP vs. USD reality**: EGP compensation at local companies has been significantly eroded by inflation (80%+ in 2023-2024). Most senior engineers target USD-paying remote roles, which provide dramatically better purchasing power stability.

**Cost of living**: Cairo remains affordable despite recent inflation. A comfortable apartment in New Cairo, Maadi, or Zamalek costs 8,000–20,000 EGP/month ($160–$400 at parallel rates). Cairo provides excellent quality of life for USD earners.

## Interview Culture and Process

**Instabug**: Technical culture is strong — the company serves global developer customers, so engineers must be world-class. Expect algorithm rounds (LeetCode medium+), system design, and behavioral rounds. Instabug's engineering blog reflects their technical values.

**Startups**: Cairo startup interviews are typically 3-4 rounds. Technical screen (algorithm/coding), system design, and culture fit. Speed matters — successful startups in Cairo move quickly through hiring processes.

**Global tech companies (Amazon, Microsoft Egypt)**: Standard international process — identical to US or UK interviews. Prepare for 4-5 rounds including behavioral (Amazon's leadership principles are applied globally), algorithm, and system design.

**Remote interviews for US/European companies**: Treat these exactly as US-based interviews. LeetCode medium/hard proficiency, system design depth, and English fluency (B2+) are requirements.

**Egyptian interview culture**: Egyptian professionals combine formal respect for interviewers with genuine intellectual engagement. Technical precision is valued. Demonstrating knowledge of MENA-specific technical challenges (Arabic language support, RTL interfaces, currency complexity) differentiates candidates for regional companies.

## Egyptian Engineer Strengths

**Arabic localization expertise**: Building software for Arabic markets (RTL layout, bidirectional text rendering, Arabic date/number formatting, transliteration) is specialized knowledge rarely found outside MENA. Global companies expanding to Arabic markets actively seek this expertise.

**Constraint-driven creativity**: Building for Egypt's market — mobile-first, diverse connectivity conditions, price-sensitive users — produces engineers skilled at resource-efficient software. This practical experience is valuable globally.

**Strong STEM education**: Cairo University, Ain Shams University, and the German University in Cairo produce strong CS graduates. Egypt also sends significant numbers of students to European and American universities, creating well-credentialed returning engineers.

**MENA payments expertise**: Deep understanding of Fawry integrations, MENA-specific payment rails, and financial regulations across Gulf and North African markets is increasingly valuable as global companies expand to these regions.

## Networking and Community

**Cairo tech hubs**: GrEEK Campus (major startup hub, multiple Cairo locations), Flat6Labs (accelerator), and AUC Venture Lab support the startup ecosystem.

**Communities**: GDG Cairo (Google Developer Group), Cairo Ruby Users Group, and Egyptian JavaScript community host regular technical events. LinkedIn Egypt Tech is active for professional networking.

**Andela acceptance**: Andela has a significant cohort of Egyptian engineers and accepts applications from Cairo. Passing Andela vetting is a globally recognized quality signal.

## Preparation Tips

1. Target remote USD-paying roles as the primary path — compensation premium is 3-5× local market
2. Develop English fluency to B2+ level — essential for international remote roles
3. Build expertise in Arabic localization and MENA-specific tech challenges
4. Practice LeetCode to medium/hard for international company interviews
5. Network through startup events at GrEEK Campus and LinkedIn Egypt Tech

Cairo's tech ecosystem is in a dynamic growth phase. Engineers who combine strong technical foundations with MENA market expertise and English fluency have exceptional career opportunities both locally and internationally.
