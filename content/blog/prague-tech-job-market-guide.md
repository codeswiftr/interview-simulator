# Prague Tech Job Market: Engineering Careers in Central Europe's Hidden Gem

Prague has quietly become one of Central Europe's most compelling tech destinations. The Czech capital combines world-class quality of life, strong technical universities (Czech Technical University, Charles University), a low cost of living relative to Western Europe, and a growing base of global tech companies that have established major engineering centers here. For engineers priced out of London or Paris, or seeking an EU base with career optionality, Prague deserves serious attention.

## The Prague Tech Landscape

**International tech companies with Prague engineering centers:**
Prague has attracted a remarkable concentration of global technology companies, primarily drawn by the high quality of Czech engineering talent and significantly lower costs than Western European cities.

- **Avast/Gen Digital** — Czech cybersecurity giant (merged with NortonLifeLock to form Gen Digital); one of Prague's largest tech employers with deep security engineering culture
- **JetBrains** — Czech-founded IDE company (IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, Kotlin); maintains strong Prague engineering presence despite HQ in Amsterdam
- **Socialbakers/Emplifi** — Social media analytics platform
- **Kiwi.com** — Travel tech unicorn; engineering for flight combination search and booking
- **Productboard** — Product management SaaS; strong engineering culture, raised over $260M
- **Mews** — Hospitality tech (property management systems); engineering-led company
- **Rohlik Group** — Online grocery delivery; logistics engineering

**Global companies with Prague offices:**
Amazon, Oracle, DXC Technology, Red Hat (IBM subsidiary), and Cisco all have meaningful engineering operations in Prague. These roles are typically software development, cloud, and enterprise software.

**Czech fintech:**
- **Twisto** (acquired by Zip) — BNPL payments
- **Zonky** — Peer-to-peer lending
- **Liftago** — Ride-hailing with engineering in Prague

**Satellite roles from Western Europe:**
Many Prague engineers work remotely for German, Dutch, or UK companies, often earning near-Western European market rates while living with Czech costs. This creates exceptional purchasing power.

## Salary Expectations

Czech salaries are substantially lower than Western Europe in absolute terms, but cost of living makes the math attractive.

**Typical ranges (2026, CZK):**
- Junior engineer (0-3 years): CZK 55,000–90,000/month (~€2,200–€3,600/month, ~€26K–€43K/year)
- Mid-level engineer (3-6 years): CZK 90,000–140,000/month (~€3,600–€5,600/month, ~€43K–€67K/year)
- Senior engineer (6+ years): CZK 140,000–200,000/month (~€5,600–€8,000/month, ~€67K–€96K/year)
- Staff/principal: CZK 200,000–280,000+ (~€96K–€134K+/year)

**International company premium:** JetBrains, Avast/Gen Digital, and US-headquartered companies pay at or near the top of these ranges. Remote roles for Western European companies can push effective earnings 30-50% higher.

**Cost of living context:** A 1-bedroom apartment in central Prague: CZK 25,000–40,000/month (~€1,000–€1,600). Prague is noticeably more expensive than Brno (Czech Republic's second city, also growing in tech) but substantially cheaper than Vienna, Munich, or Amsterdam. Engineers with mid-to-senior salaries live very comfortably.

## Interview Processes at Prague Companies

Prague tech company interviews generally follow modern European patterns — practical, less algo-puzzle-heavy than US FAANG:

**JetBrains:** Engineering culture interviews; expect deep technical knowledge, passion for developer tooling, and practical coding tasks related to IDE/compiler work. Strong emphasis on software craftsmanship.

**Avast/Gen Digital:** Security-focused technical rounds; expect knowledge of malware analysis, network security, or application security depending on the team.

**Czech startups (Kiwi, Productboard, Mews):** Take-home projects or pair programming sessions; behavioral rounds emphasizing ownership and product thinking. English interviews standard at these companies.

**For international companies (Amazon, Oracle, Red Hat Prague):** Global interview processes — same as their respective global standards, conducted in English.

## Work Authorization

**EU/EEA citizens:** No visa or work permit required. Czech Republic is an EU member — full freedom of movement.

**Non-EU citizens:** Need either an Employee Card (for employed positions) or a long-stay visa for business (90+ days). Czech Employment Authority must approve positions for non-EU nationals. Processing takes 1-4 months.

**Language:** Most Prague tech jobs at international companies are conducted in English. Czech language is helpful for daily life and at Czech companies, but English-only engineers work successfully at many Prague employers.

## Prague Life for Engineers

**Cost of living:** Prague is one of Western Europe's most affordable capitals. Groceries, dining, and entertainment cost roughly half of London or Amsterdam. Public transit is excellent and cheap. A senior engineer's salary provides a genuinely high standard of living.

**Quality of life:** Prague is stunningly beautiful (UNESCO World Heritage Site old town), has vibrant nightlife, world-class cultural institutions, and excellent restaurant and café culture. It's one of Europe's most visited tourist cities — which means great infrastructure and a cosmopolitan feel.

**Challenges:** Czech bureaucracy can be slow. Healthcare quality varies (EU health card provides basic coverage). Winter is cold and gray. Czech language, while not required in tech, is worth learning for daily life integration.

For EU citizens especially, Prague offers an exceptional combination: strong technical career opportunities, significantly lower cost of living than Western Europe, excellent quality of life, and full EU labor rights. The city is increasingly on the radar of engineers looking for an alternative to London or Berlin.
