# Salt Lake City Tech Job Market Guide: Silicon Slopes and Mountain Living

*World-class skiing, growing tech scene, and a family culture that values work-life balance.*

---

## Why Salt Lake City?

### The Silicon Slopes Story

Utah's tech corridor along I-15 has exploded:
- **2010:** A handful of startups
- **2020:** Multiple unicorns
- **2026:** Major tech hub with coast-to-coast recognition

**Why here?**
- Highly educated workforce (BYU, University of Utah)
- Lower costs than coastal hubs
- Pro-business environment
- Quality of life attracts talent

### The Pros
- **World-class outdoors:** 7 ski resorts within 45 minutes
- **Affordable:** Cost of living 15% below national average
- **Family-friendly:** Highest birth rate, lots of young families
- **Growing fast:** Tech jobs up 70% in 5 years
- **No traffic (yet):** Commutes still reasonable

### The Cons
- **Religious influence:** LDS Church presence is strong
- **Limited diversity:** Less diverse than coastal cities
- **Alcohol laws:** Quirky (improving but still weird)
- **Air quality:** Winter inversions trap pollution
- **Conservative culture:** May not suit everyone

## Neighborhoods for Tech Workers

### Salt Lake City Proper

**Downtown SLC**
- Urban, walkable
- $1,400-$2,200 for 1BR
- TRAX light rail
- Adobe HQ nearby

**Sugar House**
- Trendy, shops/restaurants
- $1,300-$2,000 for 1BR
- 10 min to downtown
- Young professional vibe

**The Avenues**
- Historic, eclectic
- $1,200-$1,900 for 1BR
- Close to Capitol, University
- More affordable

### South Valley (Tech Corridor)

**Lehi ("Silicon Slopes")**
- Heart of tech
- $1,400-$2,000 for 1BR
- Qualtrics, Adobe offices
- Longest commute to SLC

**Draper**
- Family-friendly
- $1,500-$2,200 for 2BR
- eBay, other tech
- Good schools

**Sandy/Midvale**
- Central location
- $1,400-$2,000 for 2BR
- TRAX accessible
- Balance of city/mountains

### East Side (Mountain Access)

**Millcreek/Holladay**
- Near Cottonwood canyons
- $1,500-$2,200 for 2BR
- 20 min to 4 ski resorts
- Outdoor enthusiast paradise

**Cottonwood Heights**
- Base of Big Cottonwood
- $1,600-$2,400 for 2BR
- Ski-in morning culture
- Higher cost, worth it for skiers

## The Silicon Slopes Culture

### What Makes It Different

**"Work Hard, Ski Harder"**
- Powder days are legitimate reasons to be late
- Many companies have flexible hours
- 7:00am meetings so people can ski afternoons

**Family-First**
- Parental leave is generous
- Family events at work common
- Kids welcome at some offices

**Mission-Driven**
- Many companies founded by LDS returned missionaries
- Service culture
- Values alignment matters

### Networking

**Silicon Slopes Summit:**
- Annual conference (January)
- Major tech event
- Networking central

**Meetups:**
- Silicon Slopes community
- Utah JS
- SLC DevOps Days
- Various product/startup groups

### Work Culture

**Mormon Influence (Even for Non-Mormons):**
- No coffee at some offices (changing)
- Clean language norms
- Service/volunteer culture
- Generally conservative

**Startup Energy:**
- Young workforce
- Growth mindset
- Risk-tolerant investors

## Relocating to SLC

### From Coastal Cities

**Culture adjustments:**
- Earlier dinners (7pm vs 8-9pm)
- More family-centric social scene
- Less nightlife, more outdoor recreation
- Religious culture (even for non-religious)

**What you'll love:**
- Buying a house is actually possible
- World-class skiing
- No traffic (compared to SF/Seattle)
- Friendly people

### Getting Here

**Relocation packages:**
- Qualtrics, Adobe: Standard corporate packages
- Startups: Often $0, negotiate cash
- Remote: Live here, work anywhere

**Best time to move:**
- Spring (April-May): Perfect weather
- Fall (September-October): Great for settling in before ski season
- Avoid: January (inversions), July (100°F+)
