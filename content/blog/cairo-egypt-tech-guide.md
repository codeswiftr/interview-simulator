# Cairo Tech Scene Guide: Software Engineering Opportunities in Egypt

Cairo is quietly becoming one of Africa's most dynamic tech hubs. With a population of over 21 million, a young and rapidly growing developer community, and increasing investment from regional and global tech companies, Egypt's capital offers meaningful opportunities for software engineers — whether you're a local graduate or a diaspora engineer considering a return.

## The Cairo Tech Ecosystem

Egypt's tech industry has evolved significantly over the past decade. Government-backed initiatives like the Information Technology Industry Development Agency (ITIDA) have attracted outsourcing contracts worth billions of dollars, while homegrown startups have raised record rounds of funding.

The ecosystem clusters around two main areas: **New Cairo** (particularly the Smart Village tech park) and **Maadi**, which hosts many multinational company offices. Downtown Cairo and the Zamalek district also have a growing number of co-working spaces and startup offices.

**Key sectors driving hiring:**
- Fintech (Paymob, Fawry, Telda)
- E-commerce and logistics (Jumia, Cartona, Capiter)
- Edtech and healthtech (Vezeeta, Elmenus)
- IT services and outsourcing (Vodafone Egypt, Orange, IBM)
- Global companies with Cairo engineering centers (Amazon, Microsoft, Valeo)

## Top Employers for Software Engineers

**Global tech companies with Cairo offices:** Amazon Web Services, Microsoft, Valeo (automotive software), Mentor Graphics, and Siemens all maintain significant engineering presence in Cairo. These roles come with competitive, internationally-aligned packages.

**Regional and Egyptian tech companies:** Fawry (the pioneer of Egypt's digital payments), Paymob, and Instabug (which builds mobile app monitoring tools used globally) are among the most respected engineering employers in the local market.

**IT services firms:** Large system integrators and outsourcing companies like Raya, Xceed, and IBM Egypt hire hundreds of engineers annually and often provide good entry-level opportunities.

## Salary Expectations

Salaries in Cairo vary widely depending on whether you're working for a local company, a regional firm, or a multinational.

| Experience Level | Local Company (EGP/month) | Multinational (EGP/month) |
|---|---|---|
| Junior (0–2 years) | 8,000–18,000 | 20,000–35,000 |
| Mid-level (3–5 years) | 20,000–40,000 | 40,000–70,000 |
| Senior (6+ years) | 45,000–80,000 | 80,000–150,000+ |

Note: Egypt has experienced significant currency fluctuations. Many engineers at multinationals negotiate USD-denominated salaries or USD-linked packages to hedge against currency risk.

## The Interview Process in Cairo

**For local Egyptian companies,** interviews tend to be less structured than in Western tech firms. Expect a mix of technical questions (data structures, algorithms, system design basics) combined with practical project-based discussions. Coding challenges on a whiteboard or shared screen are common, but rigorous LeetCode-style algorithmic rounds are less universal than at FAANG companies.

**For multinationals and global-facing startups,** the process mirrors what you'd find in the US or Europe: screening call, technical phone interview, a take-home or live coding round, and a final loop with system design and behavioral interviews.

**Practical tips:**
- Egyptian interviewers often appreciate candidates who explain their thought process clearly in Arabic — switching languages mid-conversation is common and accepted
- Relationship and referral-based hiring is extremely important; warm introductions carry significant weight
- Be prepared to discuss past projects in depth — portfolio work matters here

## Developer Community and Networking

Cairo has an active developer community. Key events and groups include:

- **GDG Cairo** (Google Developer Groups) — regular meetups and DevFest annual conference
- **Cairo Hack** — local hackathons
- **Flat6Labs** — startup accelerator with regular community events
- **LinkedIn and Facebook groups** — extremely active for job postings and referrals (this is Egypt-specific; Facebook Groups remain a primary professional network here)

## Quality of Life for Engineers

Cairo is a dense, energetic city with a high cost of living relative to Egyptian average wages, but genuinely affordable by global standards. Housing, food, and transportation are inexpensive for engineers earning mid-to-senior salaries. Traffic is a major quality-of-life factor — many engineers prefer to work near where they live or negotiate remote/hybrid arrangements.

The tech community tends to be young, social, and internationally-minded. English proficiency is high among developers, particularly those who studied at the American University in Cairo (AUC), Cairo University's engineering faculty, or Ain Shams University.

## Getting Ready to Interview

If you're targeting Cairo's multinational tech companies, treat your preparation the same way you would for a global tech interview:

1. Practice algorithmic problems (arrays, trees, graphs, dynamic programming)
2. Study system design fundamentals (distributed systems, databases, caching)
3. Prepare behavioral stories using the STAR method
4. Review your projects — Cairo interviewers often drill deep into what you've actually built

For local companies and startups, lean more heavily on demonstrating practical skills: GitHub portfolio, past projects, and frameworks you've worked with (React, Node.js, Django, Spring Boot, and Flutter are all in high demand).

Cairo's tech scene rewards engineers who are adaptable, multilingual, and eager to operate in a fast-moving market. If you're willing to navigate the city's unique rhythms, the opportunities are genuinely compelling.
