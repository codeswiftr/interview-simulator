# Melbourne Tech Job Market: Companies, Salaries, and the Startup Ecosystem

Melbourne has quietly built one of the strongest technology ecosystems in the Asia-Pacific region. Often overshadowed by Sydney in national headlines, Melbourne's tech scene has distinct characteristics that make it particularly attractive for certain kinds of engineers: a stronger presence of product companies (vs. financial services), a more affordable cost of living relative to compensation, and a startup ecosystem that punches above its weight for a city of its size.

## The Tech Landscape

Melbourne's technology employment divides into three tiers:

**Global tech companies** with significant Melbourne engineering presence:
- **Seek** — one of Australia's largest tech employers, engineering hub for their job marketplace platform
- **REA Group** — real estate tech, heavy engineering culture, well-regarded employer
- **Carsales** — automotive marketplace, strong engineering team
- **Xero** — accounting software, has Melbourne offices alongside Wellington and Sydney
- **Atlassian** — historically Sydney-based but with Melbourne presence; remote-friendly
- **Canva** — Sydney-headquartered, remote roles accessible from Melbourne

**Mid-size product companies**:
- **Culture Amp** — people analytics platform, Melbourne-founded, Series F, engineering-forward culture
- **Buildkite** — CI/CD tooling, small but excellent engineering reputation
- **SafetyCulture** — operations platform, grew rapidly; primary presence in Sydney but Melbourne hiring
- **Airwallex** — fintech unicorn, Melbourne-founded, global scale

**Financial services and enterprise**:
- ANZ, NAB, and Commonwealth Bank all have Melbourne engineering hubs focused on digital transformation
- MYOB — accounting software, large Melbourne engineering presence
- Seek Investments portfolio companies

## Salary Benchmarks (AUD, 2026)

Salaries vary significantly between financial services and product companies. The figures below reflect total cash compensation (base + bonus) before tax:

| Level | Financial Services | Product Companies | FAANG-equivalent |
|-------|-------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Junior (0–2 yrs) | $80,000–$100,000 | $90,000–$115,000 | $110,000–$130,000 |
| Mid-level (2–5 yrs) | $110,000–$140,000 | $120,000–$155,000 | $145,000–$185,000 |
| Senior (5–8 yrs) | $140,000–$175,000 | $155,000–$200,000 | $190,000–$240,000 |
| Staff/Principal | $180,000–$220,000 | $200,000–$270,000 | $260,000–$340,000 |
| Engineering Manager | $170,000–$210,000 | $185,000–$250,000 | $230,000–$300,000 |

Note: FAANG-equivalent roles often include RSU grants that can add significant additional compensation. Melbourne-based engineers at US-headquartered companies (Atlassian, Canva, SEEK's global businesses) increasingly receive US-benchmarked equity packages.

**Superannuation note**: Australia mandates 11.5% superannuation contributions on top of salary (the "11% Super" you see in job ads). When comparing offers, ensure you're comparing the same components — some roles quote base + super, others quote base only.

## Tech Neighborhoods and Working Culture

Melbourne's tech companies cluster in several districts:

**CBD (Central Business District)**: Most financial services engineering hubs (ANZ, NAB), Seek's headquarters, and REA Group. Professional, transit-accessible, traditional office culture.

**Cremorne / Richmond**: Dubbed "Silicon Yarra," this is Melbourne's startup neighborhood. Culture Amp, Buildkite, Airwallex, and dozens of smaller startups are headquartered here. Walkable, cafe-dense, younger culture. If you want startup energy in Melbourne, this is where it concentrates.

**Southbank / Docklands**: Enterprise tech and government digital agencies. Less startup energy, more stable employment.

Melbourne has a strong coffee culture and the office return post-pandemic has been more successful than in many global cities. Most Melbourne tech companies expect 2–3 days per week in-office, with genuine flexibility on the remaining days. Pure remote is less common than in the US or UK.

## Melbourne vs. Sydney: Which Market to Target?

This is the most common question from engineers considering relocation within Australia:

**Melbourne advantages**:
- More affordable housing (median house price roughly 20–25% lower than Sydney)
- Stronger product company ecosystem (Culture Amp, Seek, REA Group, Airwallex)
- Melbourne CBD is more compact and walkable; shorter average commute
- Larger university ecosystem (Melbourne, Monash, RMIT) feeding engineering talent

**Sydney advantages**:
- Higher absolute salaries (roughly 10–15% premium at senior levels)
- Stronger fintech presence (Afterpay, Zip, Macquarie Digital)
- More APAC headquarters of global tech companies
- Larger overall job market volume

For most engineers, Melbourne offers a better quality-of-life-to-compensation ratio. For engineers prioritizing maximum compensation, Sydney's fintech sector or APAC headquarters roles edge ahead.

## The Startup Ecosystem

Melbourne has a healthy Series A–C startup ecosystem. Key characteristics:

- **Fintech strength**: Airwallex ($5.5B+ valuation), Assembly Payments, Paytron
- **SaaS and B2B**: Culture Amp, Buildkite, Ento, and dozens of Series A companies
- **Deep tech**: Strong university-to-startup pipeline through Melbourne and Monash research commercialization
- **Government support**: LaunchVic is Victoria's startup agency, providing grants and programs for early-stage companies

The Melbourne startup scene lacks the density and capital of San Francisco or London, but it's self-sustaining. Seed funding is accessible; Series A and beyond typically involves bringing in US or Singapore-based VCs.

## Visa and Immigration

For international tech talent seeking to work in Melbourne:

**Temporary Skill Shortage (TSS) — Subclass 482**: Employer-sponsored work visa. Most software engineering roles are eligible under the Medium-term stream (4-year visa, pathway to PR). You'll need employer sponsorship.

**Employer Nomination Scheme (ENS) — Subclass 186**: Permanent residency via employer sponsorship. Typically available after 2–3 years on TSS.

**Skilled Independent — Subclass 189**: Points-tested permanent residency. Software engineers score well due to occupational demand. Requires an invitation from the government (expression of interest system).

**Global Talent — Subclass 858**: Fast-tracked PR for highly skilled professionals. Tech sector is a designated priority area. Requires a nominator (senior figure in your field) and evidence of exceptional achievement.

The Australian tech community is international and welcoming. English proficiency requirements for visa purposes are a standard part of the process; the actual workplace culture in Melbourne tech is highly multicultural.

## Making the Move

Melbourne consistently ranks among the world's most livable cities. The technology job market is mature, the talent pool is skilled, and the work-life balance expectations align more with European norms than US startup culture. Engineers who value quality of life alongside technical challenge consistently report Melbourne as one of the best places to build a long-term technology career.
