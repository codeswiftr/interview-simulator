# Toronto Tech Guide: Software Engineering Jobs in Canada's Silicon Valley

Toronto is Canada's largest tech hub and one of North America's fastest-growing tech cities. With favorable immigration pathways, lower cost of living than US tech hubs, a diverse talent pool, and a growing concentration of major employers, Toronto is an increasingly attractive destination for software engineers globally.

## Toronto's Tech Landscape

Toronto's tech ecosystem spans multiple sectors:

**Established majors:** Shopify (Ottawa-founded, large Toronto presence), Wealthsimple, Hootsuite, and Wave Financial are among Canada's strongest tech companies. TD Bank, RBC, and BMO have significant tech centers for fintech and digital transformation.

**International HQs:** Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, and Apple all have Toronto engineering offices. Google Toronto is a significant engineering hub. Uber's Advanced Technologies Group (ATG) did autonomous vehicle research here.

**AI ecosystem:** The Vector Institute (connected to Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio's research lineage) and University of Toronto's ML program make Toronto a genuine AI research hub. Companies like Layer6 AI (acquired by TD), Cohere, and Integrate.ai call Toronto home.

**Scale-ups:** Points (loyalty commerce), Ritual, Properly, Setter, and League Health are among the active scale-up scene.

## Salaries

Software engineering salaries in Toronto (CAD, 2025):

| Level | Salary Range |
|-------|-------------|
| Junior (0-2 years) | CAD $70K-$90K |
| Mid-level (2-5 years) | CAD $90K-$130K |
| Senior (5+ years) | CAD $130K-$180K |
| Staff/Principal | CAD $180K-$250K |
| Engineering Manager | CAD $150K-$220K |

US-headquartered companies (Shopify, Google, Amazon) pay the upper end. The CAD/USD conversion reduces purchasing power parity vs. US salaries, but cost of living (especially compared to San Francisco or New York) partially offsets this. Ontario income tax is progressive with a top rate around 53% combined federal + provincial — factor this into take-home calculations.

## Immigration: Express Entry

Canada's Express Entry system is one of the most favorable immigration pathways for skilled tech workers globally.

**Federal Skilled Worker (FSW):** Points-based on age, education, language (IELTS/TEF), work experience, and job offer. Software engineers with CS degrees and English proficiency typically score well (400-500 CRS points). Draws happen regularly.

**Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) — Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program (OINP):** Ontario nominates candidates in specific occupations. A provincial nomination adds 600 CRS points — near-guaranteeing an ITA (Invitation to Apply).

**Global Talent Stream (GTS):** Fast-track work permit for tech workers (typically 2 weeks processing). Your employer must be approved and the role must meet skill requirements. Standard software engineer and data scientist roles qualify.

Processing times: GTS work permits in 2 weeks; Express Entry PR applications in 6-12 months.

## Interview Culture

Toronto tech interviews generally mirror US processes, with some distinctions:

**Less intense than FAANG US:** Most Toronto companies don't run 6-7 round interview loops. 3-5 rounds is typical. Take-home assignments are common at many companies.

**Canadian politeness:** Interviewers tend to be collaborative and friendly. This can make it harder to gauge how you're doing — Canadians are unlikely to give you direct negative feedback in the moment.

**Shopify-specific:** Shopify has a distinctive interview culture. They value "mission-driven" candidates who believe in empowering entrepreneurs. Their technical rounds cover architecture and coding; they also assess alignment with their "build-over-buy" and "be a craftsperson" values.

## Tech Neighborhoods

- **King West / Financial District:** Major bank tech centers, RBC, TD, BMO
- **Waterfront / East Bayfront:** Google, Sidewalk Labs legacy space, growing tech cluster
- **Midtown:** Startups, creative agencies, some scale-ups
- **MaRS Discovery District:** Innovation hub, biotech and deeptech focused
- **Distillery District:** Creative tech, agencies

## Cost of Living

Toronto is expensive by Canadian standards but notably cheaper than San Francisco, New York, or London. A 1-bedroom apartment in central Toronto: CAD $2,200-$2,800/month. The suburbs offer lower rents with good transit connectivity (GO Transit).

## Community

Toronto has active tech communities: Shopify Unite alumni network, Toronto Machine Learning Summit, FWD50 (government tech conference), and active Python, JS, and Go meetup scenes. The DMZ (York University) and MaRS Innovation are key community centers.

For international candidates: Toronto's multiculturalism makes it welcoming. 51% of Toronto residents were born outside Canada — the diversity is genuine, not cosmetic, and reflects in workplace culture.
