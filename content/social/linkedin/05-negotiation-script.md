# LinkedIn Post: Negotiation Script

**Theme:** Salary Negotiation

---

**The most expensive sentence in your career:**

"I'll accept."

Don't say it.

---

**Negotiation isn't about getting more money.**

It's about getting what you're worth.

And most engineers leave $15,000-50,000 on the table because they don't negotiate.

---

**The Scripts:**

**The "Thank You, But..." Script:**

"Thank you so much for this offer. I'm really excited about the role and the team. Based on my experience and market rates for this level, I was hoping we could discuss the compensation. I was thinking $X total compensation."

---

**The Total Compensation Angle:**

Don't just negotiate base salary.

RSUs. Sign-on bonus. Learning budget. Vacation days.

"I understand the base is $Y, but with the RSU refresh rate and total package, can we adjust to $Z?"

---

**The Flexible Pivot:**

"I know the budget might be tight on base, but if we could increase the sign-on bonus or accelerate the RSU vesting schedule, that would help me make a decision."

---

**The Walk-Away Number:**

Know it before you start.

What's the minimum you'll accept?

Once you have it, don't go below it.

---

**Handling "No":**

If they can't move on money?

Ask about:
- Vacation days
- Learning budget
- Remote work flexibility
- Title
- Promotion timeline

---

**The uncomfortable truth:**

Asking for more is uncomfortable.

Not asking is expensive.

$5,000 now compounds to $500,000 over 20 years.
