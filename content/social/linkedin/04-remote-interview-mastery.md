# LinkedIn Post: Remote Interview Best Practices

**Theme:** Remote/Virtual Interview Tips

---

**Remote work is here to stay.**

Your screen is your stage.

Here's how to master it:

---

**The Technical Setup:**

Audio > Video.

Every time.

A $20 microphone beats a $2000 camera.

Why? Because bad audio is physically painful to listen to.

---

**The Eye Level Rule:**

Stop propping your laptop on books.

It gives the "up-the-nose" angle.

Get a $20 monitor stand or put your laptop on a box.

Eye level = professional.

---

**The Presence Hack:**

Look at the CAMERA, not the screen.

Your natural instinct is to look at the interviewer's face.

Fight it.

Looking at the lens = looking them in the eyes.

---

**The Energy Gap:**

On video, you need 20% more animation to read as "normal."

Flat delivery on Zoom = boring on video.

Be slightly animated. Slightly louder. Slightly more expressive.

---

**The Digital Workflow:**

Close private tabs before screen sharing.

Use a dedicated "interview browser" with nothing but documentation open.

Know your whiteboard shortcuts.

---

**Recovery:**

Internet drops. Mic fails. Things go wrong.

Your response is being evaluated.

Stay calm. "Let me switch to my phone hotspot" is a leadership moment.
