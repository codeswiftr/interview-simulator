# LinkedIn Post: 7 Deadly Sins of Tech Interviews

**Theme:** Interview Mistakes

---

**You have the skills. You have the resume. No offer.**

Here are the 7 mistakes I see senior engineers make in interviews:

---

**1. The "We" Trap**

Stop saying "we shipped this feature."

Interviewers want to hear YOUR contribution.

Say "I architected X" not "we did Y."

---

**2. Monotone Delivery**

Remote interviews read low energy as lack of interest.

You need 20% more animation than feels natural.

Talk like you're excited. Because you should be.

---

**3. The Silent Coder**

Technical interview? They're not just grading the answer.

They're grading how you think.

If you're silent for 5 minutes, you're failing.

---

**4. Bad-Mouthing Past Employers**

Even if your last boss was terrible?

Say: "There were cultural differences" not "My manager was incompetent."

It shows you can't handle conflict professionally.

---

**5. Ignoring Non-Technical Cues**

System design interview? Ask clarifying questions.

Not asking = not curious = not senior material.

---

**6. The "Everything is Great" Lie**

"I've never had a conflict" = "I have zero self-awareness"

Everyone has conflict. Show how you handled it.

---

**7. Poor Remote Setup**

Bad audio is the fastest way to lose points.

$20 on a microphone > $2000 laptop.

---

**The Cringe Test:**

Record yourself. Watch it. Win.
