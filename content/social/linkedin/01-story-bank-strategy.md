# LinkedIn Post: The "Story Bank" Strategy

**Theme:** Behavioral Interview Prep

---

**The "hidden" filter no one talks about in technical interviews:**

You can crush the coding challenge and still get rejected.

Why?

Because the behavioral interview is where senior engineers go to die.

---

**The Problem:**

Memorizing 50 answers to 50 behavioral questions is the worst strategy I see candidates use.

Here's what actually works:

**The "Story Bank" Framework**

Instead of 50 answers, you need 5-7 versatile career moments that can answer 10+ different questions each.

I call it "Multi-Threading" your stories.

---

**One story. Ten questions it answers:**

- "Tell me about a technical challenge" → The migration that broke production
- "Describe a conflict with a coworker" → That same migration debate
- "Give an example of leadership" → Advocating for the architectural change
- "When did you fail?" → The rollback we had to do

Same story. Different angles.

---

**The 90-Second Rule:**

Keep each story under 90 seconds.

Focus on YOUR action, not "we did this."

Quantify the result.

---

**How to build yours:**

1. List your "Hero Moments" (the hard stuff you solved)
2. Categorize by signal (grit, collaboration, technical depth)
3. Practice until it sounds natural, not rehearsed

---

**The uncomfortable truth:**

You don't have a behavioral interview problem.

You have a "I can't articulate what I actually did" problem.
