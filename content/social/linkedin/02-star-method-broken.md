# LinkedIn Post: STAR Method is Broken

**Theme:** Behavioral Interview Technique

---

**The STAR method is dead.**

Not because it doesn't work.

Because everyone uses it. And interviewers are sick of it.

---

**Here's the problem:**

Candidate: "So, I was working on this project where we had this situation... and then we did this action... and it resulted in this outcome."

Interviewer: *checks phone*

You're spending 60 seconds on "Situation" when they only care about "Action."

---

**STAR 2.0:**

**S/T (Context):** 15 seconds max. High stakes only.
**A (The Meat):** 70% of your answer. Show reasoning and trade-offs.
**R (Result):** Quantify it. "Reduced latency by 40%."

That's it.

---

**For Senior/Staff roles, add one more:**

**L (Learned):** What would you do differently next time?

This is where you show self-awareness and growth mindset.

---

**The "Robot" problem:**

If your answers sound scripted, you've already lost.

The goal isn't to memorize.

It's to have 3-4 powerful stories you can tell naturally, adapted to whatever question comes your way.

---

**Stop practicing answers.**

Start practicing storytelling.
