**Send trigger:** 3-4 days after Email #2

---

**Body:**

Hey {first_name},

"Tell me about a time you handled a difficult stakeholder."

Behavioral questions are notorious for tripping up even the most technically brilliant candidates. They're designed to assess your soft skills, problem-solving approach, and how you'd fit into a team.

The secret? The **STAR Method**:
*   **S**ituation: Set the scene.
*   **T**ask: Describe your responsibility.
*   **A**ction: Explain what you did.
*   **R**esult: Quantify the outcome.

It sounds simple, but perfecting a compelling STAR story under pressure is tough. Most people ramble, focus too much on the situation, or forget the critical "Result."

**This is where your AI coach shines.**
Interview Simulator's AI specifically analyzes your answers for STAR adherence. It will tell you precisely if you're missing the "Result" or if your "Action" isn't clear enough. It even prompts you with follow-up questions to help you refine your story in real-time.

Stop guessing if your behavioral answers are hitting the mark. Start practicing with precision.
